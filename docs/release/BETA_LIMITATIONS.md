# Beta limitations

- Hawelly coordinates transfers through internal staff; it does not provide
  crypto settlement, wallets, float, agent commissions, or autonomous payout.
- External associates are directory contacts coordinated through approved
  external channels. They cannot sign in to an agent portal.
- Quotes expire and may change before acceptance. Only the accepted immutable
  quote and its funding instructions govern an in-product transfer.
- A submitted funding reference or receipt is pending until authorized staff
  review it and separately confirm receipt of funds.
- A payout report is an operational record. Sender/recipient confirmation and
  dispute handling remain available; exceptional completion/refund actions
  require authorized, reasoned administration.
- Notifications are best effort. Testers should check the transfer timeline and
  contact support for time-sensitive discrepancies.
- Evidence uses private host storage in this release. The deployment owner must
  back it up separately from PostgreSQL and enforce the approved retention and
  access policy.
- Android distribution is outside an app store during controlled beta unless a
  later release plan says otherwise. Testers must install only the published
  digest-matched artifact.
- Country, currency, payout method, evidence, transfer-limit, and SLA support is
  runtime-configured and may be narrower than the interface examples.
- The beta is not authorization to use real funds, personal data, or regulated
  corridors beyond the operator's explicit legal/compliance approval.
